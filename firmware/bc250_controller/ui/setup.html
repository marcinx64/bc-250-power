BC-250 power controller

This controller switches your BC-250 PC on and off. To use it from your home network, it needs to join your WiFi. This takes about a minute.

Your WiFi password is stored only on the controller.

This controller has no password yet. Set one first; it protects the controller on your network.

Forgot it? Hold the front button while powering the board up, for 10 s. The board beeps and flashes its LED 5 times and erases the password and the WiFi settings.
