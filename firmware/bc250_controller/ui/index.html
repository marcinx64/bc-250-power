Wake method

  • BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy): BLE game controllers, found by Scan.
  • 2.4G/BT: devices with a 2.4G USB dongle and classic Bluetooth controllers through their host.

Switching restarts the controller; the BC-250’s power state is unaffected.
